## Reply to customer

> Hi Marco,
>
> Thanks for getting in touch. Here's what to do:
>
> 1. Open billing settings. Go to Settings → Billing. Only the workspace owner can see this page.
> 2. Replace the card. Choose "Update payment method", enter the new card and save.
> 3. Retry the failed payment. Choose "Retry payment" on the open invoice. The retry usually completes within a few minutes.
>
> These steps come from our guide "Updating your payment method" (KB-201). If they don't solve the problem, reply here and tell us what you see.
