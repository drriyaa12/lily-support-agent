Use this guide when a customer has two-step verification turned on and no longer has the phone or app that generates their codes.

## Steps
1. **Use a backup code.** On the verification screen, choose "Use a backup code" and enter one of the ten codes saved when two-step verification was set up. Each code works once.
2. **Use a trusted device.** If the customer is still signed in on another computer or tablet, they can approve the sign-in from Settings → Security → Trusted devices.

## When to escalate
If the customer has no backup codes and no trusted device, do not try to bypass verification or change the account email. Escalate to Account Security, who verify identity before removing two-step verification.
