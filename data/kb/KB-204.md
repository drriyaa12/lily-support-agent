Workspace owners can cancel or move to a smaller plan at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and all files stay available on the Free plan up to its storage limit.

## Steps
1. **Open the plan page.** Go to Settings → Billing → Plan.
2. **Choose the new plan or cancel.** Select a smaller plan, or choose "Cancel subscription" to move to Free at the end of the period.
3. **Check the confirmation email.** Owners receive an email with the date the change takes effect.
