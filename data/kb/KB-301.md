Invitations are sent from invites@quillstack.example and stay valid for 7 days.

## Steps
1. **Check spam and quarantine.** Ask the invited person to search their inbox, spam folder and any company quarantine for "Quillstack".
2. **Resend the invitation.** In Members → Pending invitations, choose "Resend". Resending creates a new 7-day link.
3. **Check allowed email domains.** If the workspace restricts sign-ups by domain (Settings → Security → Allowed domains), the invited address must use an allowed domain.

## When to escalate
If the invitation still does not arrive after these steps, escalate to Technical Support with the invited email address.
