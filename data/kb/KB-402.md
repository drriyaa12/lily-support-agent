Requests to delete an account, erase personal data, or receive a copy of personal data are handled by the Privacy team, who verify the requester's identity first.

## When to escalate
Always escalate. Acknowledge the request, do not delete anything or promise a completion date, and do not ask the customer to send identity documents by email.
