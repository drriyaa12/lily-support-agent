Signs of a compromised account include sign-in alerts from unfamiliar locations, a password or email change the customer did not make, unknown sessions, or files shared without permission.

## Steps
1. **Change the password now.** If the customer can still sign in, they should change their password from Settings → Security.
2. **Sign out of all sessions.** Settings → Security → Sessions → "Sign out everywhere" ends every active session.

## When to escalate
Always escalate suspected compromise to Trust & Safety, even if the customer has already changed their password. Do not troubleshoot, restore files, or confirm account details in the reply.
