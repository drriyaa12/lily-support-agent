Subscription charges can be refunded within 14 days of the charge date. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Steps
1. **Confirm the invoice number.** Invoice numbers look like INV-123456 and are listed under Settings → Billing → Invoices.
2. **Request the refund.** Next to the invoice, choose "Request refund". Requests inside the refund window are approved automatically.
3. **Allow 5–10 business days.** The refund appears on the statement within 5–10 business days, depending on the bank.

## When to escalate
Requests outside the refund window, partial refunds and disputed charges need approval from the Billing team.
