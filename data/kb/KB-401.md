Workspace owners and admins can export all documents and files as a ZIP archive at any time, on every plan.

## Steps
1. **Start the export.** Go to Settings → Workspace → Export data and choose "Create export".
2. **Wait for the email.** Large workspaces can take up to 24 hours. An email arrives when the archive is ready.
3. **Download within 7 days.** The download link in the email expires after 7 days.
