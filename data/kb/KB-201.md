Workspace owners can change the card used for the subscription at any time. When a payment fails, the workspace keeps working for 7 days while the owner updates the card.

## Steps
1. **Open billing settings.** Go to Settings → Billing. Only the workspace owner can see this page.
2. **Replace the card.** Choose "Update payment method", enter the new card and save.
3. **Retry the failed payment.** Choose "Retry payment" on the open invoice. The retry usually completes within a few minutes.

## When to escalate
If the retry fails with a new card, escalate to Billing. Never ask the customer to send card details by email or chat.
