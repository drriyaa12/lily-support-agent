**When am I charged?** Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month. Annual plans are charged once a year on the day you subscribed.

**Is there a money-back guarantee?** Yes. Every paid plan can be refunded in full within 30 days of the charge.

**Which currencies do you accept?** Prices are shown and charged in US dollars or euros, depending on the billing country.
