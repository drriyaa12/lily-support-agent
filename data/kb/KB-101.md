Use this guide when a customer cannot sign in because they forgot their password or the reset email does not arrive. Password resets are self-serve.

## Steps
1. **Request a reset link.** On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password?" and enter the email address on the account.
2. **Check spam and filters.** Reset emails come from no-reply@quillstack.example and can be caught by spam folders or company mail filters.
3. **Use the newest link within 30 minutes.** Each new request cancels earlier links, and a link expires 30 minutes after it is sent.
4. **Try a private browser window.** Browser extensions sometimes block the reset page from loading.

## When to escalate
If no reset email arrives after all steps, escalate to Account Support. The address may be on the email suppression list after an earlier bounce, which only staff can clear.
